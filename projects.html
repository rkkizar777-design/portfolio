LostNote.

LostNote is an offline-first notes app that lives entirely on your device. Notes, photos and PDFs stay local, while phone ↔ PC sync happens in seconds over P2P WebRTC with a single 5-digit code. Archive and trash travel exactly as they are, and a PIN-locked vault keeps sensitive notes separate. Zero servers, zero accounts, zero tracking.

Offline-firstNotes, photos and PDFs live on the device. Nothing ever leaves it.

KIZ Player.

KIZ Player is a native Android music player built from scratch in Java. It reads your offline library, sorts it into moods with on-device AI, downloads audio straight from YouTube and renders a full-screen visualizer while a track plays. Your library never leaves the device, and the app works with no signal, no account and no server.
